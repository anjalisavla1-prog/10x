10 Tourist Places to Visit in India

Discover a few of India's remarkable landmarks, cities, and landscapes.

This is a curated list of popular destinations, not an official ranking. Each place offers a different way to experience India's history, culture, or natural beauty.

1. Taj Mahal, Agra

This white-marble mausoleum was built by Mughal emperor Shah Jahan in memory of Mumtaz Mahal. Visitors come to admire its architecture and gardens.

2. Jaipur, Rajasthan

Known as the Pink City, Jaipur is celebrated for its historic palaces, colorful markets, and landmarks such as Hawa Mahal and Amber Fort.

3. Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh

One of India's oldest continuously inhabited cities, Varanasi is known for its riverside ghats, temples, and spiritual traditions along the Ganges.

4. Kerala Backwaters

A network of lagoons, canals, and lakes runs through Kerala. A boat trip is a popular way to see waterside villages and green landscapes.

5. Goa

Goa is known for its beaches, seafood, and Portuguese-influenced architecture. Old Goa's churches are recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

6. Hampi, Karnataka

The ruins of the Vijayanagara Empire sit among striking boulder-strewn landscapes. Hampi is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

7. Ladakh

This high-altitude region in the Himalayas is known for mountain scenery, Buddhist monasteries, and clear blue lakes.

8. Darjeeling, West Bengal

Darjeeling is famous for its tea gardens and views of the Himalayas. The historic Darjeeling Himalayan Railway is also a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

9. Khajuraho, Madhya Pradesh

Khajuraho's medieval temples are renowned for detailed stone carvings and are protected as a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

10. Amritsar, Punjab

Amritsar is home to the Golden Temple, the central place of worship for Sikhs and a welcoming landmark known for its community kitchen.

India has many more places to explore. Plan your visit and learn about local customs before you travel.
